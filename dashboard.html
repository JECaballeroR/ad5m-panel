    Connect your printer

    The extension talks to the printer directly on your local network. Nothing goes through the cloud.

    Where do I find these?
    1. On the printer screen, tap the gear (Settings), then the Wi‑Fi icon.
    2. Under “Network mode”, turn on “LAN only”. The check code (sometimes shown as printer ID) and the IP address appear there.
    3. The serial number is on the machine info screen (the “i” icon in Settings). It usually starts with SN.

    With LAN only on, the FlashMaker app and cloud mode stop working; Orca‑Flashforge keeps working in LAN mode. Reserve the printer’s IP in your router so it doesn’t change.
